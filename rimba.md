# Company Profile: Rimba Digital

**"Deeply Rooted in Your Moments"**

---

## 1. Ringkasan Eksekutif

Rimba Digital adalah anak perusahaan dari Rimba Wedding (est. 2001) yang bergerak di bidang Wedding Technology (WedTech). Kami melakukan transformasi dari vendor pernikahan tradisional menjadi penyedia solusi digital berbasis SaaS (Software as a Service). Rimba Digital menggabungkan warisan pengalaman selama 25 tahun dengan inovasi teknologi terkini untuk menciptakan ekosistem pernikahan yang elegan, teratur, dan berkesan.

---

## 2. Visi & Misi

### Visi
Menjadi pemimpin pasar nasional dalam orkestrasi pernikahan digital yang memadukan estetika desain tingkat tinggi dengan efisiensi teknologi.

### Misi
1. **Digitalisasi Tanpa Batas:** Mendigitalisasi pengalaman pernikahan melalui sistem undangan, manajemen tamu, dan perencanaan yang mulus.
2. **Elevasi Estetika:** Menghadirkan standar estetika premium yang belum pernah ada di industri WedTech lokal.
3. **Keamanan & Transparansi:** Menjamin keamanan dan transparansi data bagi pengantin melalui infrastruktur sistem yang solid.

---

## 3. Identitas Visual & Branding

Website harus mencerminkan kesan **Premium, Eksklusif, dan "Misterius namun Hangat"**.

## 6. Heartwood Series - Kategori Adat

### Aesthetic Direction: "Neo-Adat Noir"

**Theme: "Sacred Luxe"**
- Fusion of Indonesian adat traditions with dark luxury aesthetics
- Ancient Javanese temples illuminated by golden candlelight
- Balinese offerings in moonlit pavilion
- Sumatran ceremonial gold meets Art Deco geometry

### Color Palette

| Token | Hex | Name | Usage |
|-------|-----|------|-------|
| --bg | #0A0A08 | Deep Obsidian Black | Main backgrounds |
| --surface | #121210 | Charcoal | Cards, sections |
| --surface-2 | #1A1A17 | Dark Ash | Elevated surfaces |
| --gold | #C9A84C | Antique Gold | Primary accent |
| --gold-light | #E8D5A0 | Champagne Gold | Highlights |
| --gold-dim | #7A6330 | Aged Bronze | Subtle accents |
| --text | #F0EDE6 | Ivory | Main text |
| --text-muted | #9A9A8E | Silver Gray | Secondary text |
| --text-dim | #5A5A50 | Whisper | Tertiary text |
| --border | rgba(201,168,76,0.15) | Gold Border | Borders |

### Typography

| Font | Role | Usage |
|------|------|-------|
| Cormorant Garamond | Display | Names, headlines |
| Inter | Body | UI, paragraphs |
| EB Garamond | Script | Quotes, Arabic |

### Ornamental Design System

**Javanese Kawung Pattern** - Harmony, Unity
**Balinese Canang Sari** - Balance sacred/mundane
**Sumatran Bundo Kandis** - Royalty, strength

### Section Designs

| Section | Indonesian Name | Description |
|---------|-----------------|-------------|
| Cover | Pintu Kelola | Countdown, names with shimmer |
| Greeting | Salam Padubdos | Bismillah, guest name |
| Couple | Pasangan Suci | Photo frames, parents info |
| Event | Rincian Pelaksanaan | Map with Neo-Noir filter |
| Gallery | Kenangan Terindah | Horizontal snap scroll |
| RSVP | Konfirmasi Kehadiran | Form with gold inputs |
| Gift | Amplop Digital | Bank card copy |
| Closing | Terima Kasih | Quran verse, hashtag |

### Premium Features
- Gold shimmer animation
- Noise texture overlay
- Vignette effect
- Corner ornaments (SVG)
- Framer Motion animations
- Stagger: 0.18s per item
